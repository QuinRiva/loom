- **This project's boundaries and posture.** Loom is reliability-first at its real boundaries — session restarts, reconnects, partial streams, provider processes, user input — and guards there are load-bearing. Everywhere else it is a single-user prototype under active watch where iteration speed wins; but its control plane must stay correct under restarts and partial streams, so on that surface the severity bar is correctness over convenience.
- **Named compatibility exceptions:** the event-sourced ledgers (decode-defaulted contract fields so old snapshots load) and the upstream-sync migration lanes. Outside those, the built-in rule stands: a shim is a finding unless the brief explicitly demanded it.
- The control plane is tested deliberately — decider invariants, dispatcher rails, contract round-trips — and those tests catch real regressions: judge them for coverage of the change's risk, not for existing.
