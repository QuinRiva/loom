## Method that worked this session (reuse it)

1. `docs/upstream-sync/pull7-tools/whatismissing.sh <file> <name>...` on every
   TS2304/TS2305/TS2724 — it names which parent still declares the symbol.
2. `docs/upstream-sync/pull7-tools/restoredecl.py <rev> <file> <name>...` (new)
   extracts that declaration from the parent and appends it. It over-extracts on
   multi-line `new Set([...])` initialisers — eyeball what it appends.
3. Prop-shape errors (TS2741/TS2739 on a component) are almost always "loom's
   caller, upstream's callee". Diff the props of the callee against BOTH parents
   before touching the caller.
