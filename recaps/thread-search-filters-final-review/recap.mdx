# Ship after two decisions: the switches work, but search can now say "No threads found" for a thread that exists

**The build gives you what you asked for.** With nothing touched, the sidebar box and Cmd+K read top-level threads and reports. They leave out archived threads and sub-threads (**0 archived rows** across 20 searches I ran). Three switches change that: Sub-threads, Archived, and one project. With Sub-threads on, **9 of 10** searches whose answer is a sub-thread list it under its top-level thread, and choosing it opens it. `CI` now returns results. No plan, recap or explainer is indexed.

**Two things need your call.** The build also added two rules that make search answer "No threads found", neither asked for in your own words. One is a tuned similarity threshold, which hid a right answer on one of my runs. The other is a word check, which empties a one-word search that has a typo. And the Cmd+K project chip you picked is missing on a new-thread screen.

An item you leave unmarked takes its recommendation.

## 2. Two small cuts the orchestrator will make unless you object

- **A new option on the agents' search tool** that leaves sub-threads' own text out. Nobody asked for it, and its description rides in every agent's prompt. It lives in `apps/server/src/mcp/toolkits/workstream/defs.ts` and `apps/server/src/mcp/toolkits/workstream/handlers/threadSearch.ts`, with one test.
- **The Reset button beside the chips.** With two or three chips, clicking a lit chip does the same, and clearing the box already turns every switch off. In a narrow sidebar it is clipped to "Res". It lives in `apps/web/src/loom/ThreadSearchFilterChips.tsx` and `apps/mobile/src/features/threads/ThreadSearchFilterChips.loom.tsx`.

## 3. What I took "done" to mean

Written from your messages before I opened the build, so you can see at once if I misread you.

- You type in the sidebar box or Cmd+K and, touching nothing, get top-level threads matched on their own words and on reports. Archived threads and sub-threads stay out: "If I've archived a thread ... including it in search results by default seems counterproductive."
- You can switch sub-threads in, archived in, and narrow to one project. In Cmd+K the project switch is a chip for the current project and nothing bigger.
- With sub-threads in, a result is the sub-thread itself under its top-level thread, and choosing it opens that sub-thread.
- Short words such as `CI` find messages.
- No search over plans, recaps, intent briefs or explainers: "let's leave out all the mdx docs then." Reports stay searched, as they already were.

Three things your messages do not settle, which the build decided and I left alone. The switches return to off whenever the box is cleared or closed, so nothing is remembered between searches. The phone and iPad get the same default and switches, though you named only the sidebar and Cmd+K. With Sub-threads on, a broad search lists about 14 to 28 top-level threads in place of 50, because each brings up to three sub-thread rows.
