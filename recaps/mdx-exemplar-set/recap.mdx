# Five candidate exemplars: is this what good looks like?

Five documents were written under the rewritten MDX skills, then reviewed and tuned (facts re-checked against their sources, first screens repaired, register fixed). Three are same-subject re-authorings of documents you have already read, so the old and new versions sit side by side; one is a fresh explainer from code; one is a content-for-content port of an HTML explainer you liked. The reviewer's ranking, best to worst at closing the HTML gap: **fan-in recap → agent-started cue → sqlite read-lane → MDX pipeline explainer → document-classification port.** The port is the least finished-looking and the most useful: its fourteen in-place *Vocabulary gap —* notes are the inventory of what new blocks would have to do.

Each card below asks one thing: **Accept** = this is the bar, promote it into the skill as the shipped exemplar; **Reject** = not the bar, say what is missing; **Discuss** = annotate. An item left undecided takes its recommendation. Open the documents in the order listed; the first is the medium test.

## 3. What happens after your verdicts
