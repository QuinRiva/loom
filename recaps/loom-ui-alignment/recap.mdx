# Loom's own surfaces now follow upstream's redesign

**PR #533 restyles the parts of the app Loom added in upstream's October idiom: neutral cards, sentence-case labels, 12px metadata, and colour only where you have to act.** That covers control cards, handoff receipts, consult cards, plans and recaps, the sidebar badge, and the Workstream and Goal panels. No design-scoring rounds have run yet; having two models score every screen is the next PR.

<Callout focus title="What it means now">
  Below is the screen you see most, an orchestrator thread with its Workstream panel: four of the six changes show in that one frame. Light mode had its own contrast pass, and the faintest Loom text anywhere went from **1.74:1 to 4.62:1**. One thing stays open and is not part of this PR: the Workstream graph is still small at fit-to-view (§4). The merge question is at the bottom.
</Callout>

1. **Cards are coloured by urgency, not by the channel that sent them.** The deadlock card was green like every other card; now it has a red border. Attention and review-rework cards are amber.
2. **Routine notices are grey.** The stall nudge was the same green as the deadlock; now it is a plain card, like upstream's.
3. **The header label is a word you read first.** "ATTENTION" in small green capitals at the far right is now "Attention" beside the icon, in the card's colour. You see what kind of notice it is before what it says.
4. **Roles are plain text.** The outlined green "coder" chip is now the word coder in grey.
5. **The sidebar badge lost its outline.** `4/9 · 1!` is coloured text beside the network icon, the way upstream draws a pull-request badge.
6. **The Active now strip lost its role square.** The "RES" avatar is gone; the role is written out on the model line instead.

## 4. Workstream graph

<Columns>
  <Column label="Before">
    <Image src="shots/05-graph-before.png" alt="Graph cards with 11px titles; finished and cancelled cards faded as a whole, text included" caption="Before: part of the graph, light, preview harness, enlarged three times from fit-to-view (hence the blur)" />
  </Column>
  <Column label="After">
    <Image src="shots/05-graph-after.png" alt="Graph cards with 12px titles; finished and cancelled cards have faded fills but readable text" caption="After: the same part of the graph" />
  </Column>
</Columns>

1. **Text is one step larger on every card:** titles 12px instead of 11, the role and state line 9.5px instead of 8. Longer titles now wrap to two lines.
2. **A finished card fades its fill and border, not its text.** "Add config loader" used to print at 1.74:1 against its own card; the title is now readable and "done" shows in green.
3. **Cancelled cards work the same way** ("Abandoned experiment").

<Callout tone="warning" title="Still small at fit-to-view">
  One step larger is not enough at fit-to-view. The panel shrinks a nine-wave workstream to fit, so a 12px title is drawn at roughly 5 to 8 pixels, depending on panel width. The image below is at real size. Two ways to fix it are with you and are not in this PR. One draws less when the graph is small (titles and status colour only, details on zoom). The other opens the graph at readable size, panned to the root, instead of fitting everything.
</Callout>

## 5. Light mode

<Columns>
  <Column label="Before">
    <Image src="shots/06-light-before.png" alt="Digest card in light mode with a pale green tint, DIGEST label at 2.7:1, boxed role chips and pale uppercase status words" caption="Before: the expanded digest card, light, preview harness, enlarged 1.5 times" />
  </Column>
  <Column label="After">
    <Image src="shots/06-light-after.png" alt="Digest card in light mode on a white neutral card, Digest label at 4.7:1, plain role text and darker sentence-case status words" caption="After: the same card" />
  </Column>
</Columns>

1. **The header label passes contrast.** "DIGEST" in pale green capitals measured 2.7:1; "Digest" in grey beside the icon measures 4.7:1.
2. **The green tint over the whole card is gone;** it is a white card like upstream's.
3. **Role chips are plain text**, as in dark mode.
4. **Status words are sentence case and darker** ("finished", "gate resolved").

Across every Loom surface in the preview harness, the faintest text in light mode was **1.74:1** (the finished graph cards in §4). It is now **4.62:1**, above the 4.5:1 that small text needs.
