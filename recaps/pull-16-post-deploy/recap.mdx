**The one miss.** A coder's turn in another project was in flight at the switch. The new server resumed it at 22:27:28, and its start-of-run snapshot gave up 5.1 seconds later. Every later capture in the same folder took 1 to 2 seconds. So this is what a turn resumed in the first seconds after a restart costs: none to a few runs per deploy. I recommend accepting it and have filed nothing.
